# Atlantia

> AI-powered market research and consumer intelligence platform. Trusted by 200+ teams across Latin America and the United States.

## About

Atlantia is an AI platform founded in 2020 in Mexico City that automates market research. It combines proprietary AI models with statistical rigor to deliver brand tracking, demand analysis, concept testing, packaging testing, pricing strategy, and 10+ automated solutions. Results in days, not months.

## Business Units

- [Atlantia Insights](https://www.atlantia.ai/en/insights): AI market research platform with 10+ automated solutions
- [maia](https://www.atlantia.ai): AI platform for market research and consumer intelligence
- [Shelftia](https://www.shelftia.com): AI-driven SaaS for real-time price monitoring across retail
- [Suppi](https://www.suppi.com.mx): AI-powered grocery price comparison in Mexico
